## Style Guide for All Images

- **Character:** Orange fox, big green eyes, dark business suit, white shirt
- **Style:** 3D Pixar/Disney quality render
- **Background:** Always transparent (PNG with alpha)
- **Lighting:** Warm, slight orange rim light
- **Resolution:** At least 1024x1024px, ideally 2048px tall for hero images
- **Mood:** Professional but friendly and approachable
- **File format:** PNG (or GIF for animations)
